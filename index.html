STUDENT SUPPORT

Help Center

Answers to common questions about document requests.

How do I request a document?

Open Request Documents, select the document type, provide the purpose, and submit the form.

How do I check my request status?

Open My Requests to see your reference number and the latest status.

What if my request is rejected?

Read the staff remarks, correct the issue, and contact the registrar if you need assistance.

How long does processing take?

Processing times depend on the document and school policy. Contact your registrar for an official estimate.
